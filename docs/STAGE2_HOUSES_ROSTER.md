# Stage 2+: haunted houses, roster, special attacks, gear (what is real, what is verified)

## Haunted houses (HOUSE_01 .. HOUSE_30)
`world/Houses.java` (framework + 01-05) and `world/HousesB.java` (06-30). Every house has its own hand-written design and four Sign texts in English
(waxed, so players cannot edit them); each has a `lore_desk` (feeds the existing notes) and a chest. The seven usernames appear exactly as written.
Placement (`Houses.place`): never over player-made blocks (`Structures.isClear`), no water/ocean/river, terrain spread <= 3, >= 40 blocks from any recorded
structure and >= 160 from another house, each house once per dimension, recorded in `StructureData` (so `/lastsignal locate HOUSE_nn` works).
Event `env_haunted_house` (rare; config `hauntedHouseFrequency`, 0 disables): houses unlock with the story stage (6 per stage); HOUSE_30 only after HOUSE_29 exists.
Progression chain: HOUSE_29 (Last Camp) holds the `red_key`; HOUSE_30's entrance is a `sealed_door_block` that opens with it. `/lastsignal place HOUSE_nn` places one in front of an operator.
## Roster
17 original horrors + 12 new (Black Witness, Tall Black ~3 m, Borrowed Skin, Drowned Choir, Hanging Man, Salt Mother, Radio Shepherd, Glass Walker, Candle Bearer,
Paper Man, Stair Thing, Moon Watcher) + White Presence = 30. New ones have GeckoLib rigs (bones/animations generated by tools/monsters.py), behaviours by archetype, spawn eggs,
and 12 stage-gated events. `tools/generate_assets.py` must NOT be re-run in full (it regenerates all dimension sounds/textures and overwrites sounds.json/lang);
the new assets were kept and everything else restored.
## Special attacks
`entity/SpecialAttacks.java`: 14 moves (lunge, ankle grab, tremor slam, step behind, shatter, pull down, wide bite, choir wail, echo cry, stare, black stare, snuff, salt wall, dread).
Cooldown, range band, minimum story stage, 1-in-3 roll, line of sight; status effects/impulses/small damage only; no block changes; config `enableSpecialAttacks`.
## Gear
Lantern Warden armor (full set: Darkness immunity, -15% damage from creatures), Salt Iron Sword (frightens), Echo Dagger (backstab bonus), Signal Halberd (bonus vs creatures); recipes in data, assets via tools/gear.py.

## Director rules (design lock)
`event/Director.java`: scopes PLAYER / LOCAL_GROUP / WORLD / STORY derived from each event (73 events: 37 / 26 / 0 / 10; no event is WORLD-wide yet), four phases (Suspicion, Observation, Pursuit, Final Signal)
derived from the story state (Final Signal needs `signal_core_unlocked` or a defeated boss), cooldowns shared per scope (friends in one 64-block region share LOCAL_GROUP cooldowns),
Pursuit events blocked before Observation unless item-gated, cleanup of hunting creatures plus quiet periods (240 s after death, 180 s after respawn, 60 s after a world change), `/lastsignal status` shows the phase.
CI runs every event once on a fake player: no exception escaped (32 fired, 41 false alarms by design when no suitable spot exists).

## Stage 3 additions
- **Spawn anchor** (`event/SpawnAnchor.java`): AUTO / PERSONAL (respawn point) / WORLD (shared spawn). Scattering placements (ground, forest edge, high ground, mountain, rooftop, open field) look around the anchor within `spawnAnchorRadius` (48) with chance `spawnAnchorBias` (0.7), only if the player is within radius+64 blocks. Never teleports anyone, never loads chunks, falls back to the player-centred search, and skips the event when nothing safe exists. Not applied in mod dimensions.
- **Escalating distance**: each sighting near the spawn raises `PlayerStory.sightings` (saved); the next anchored sighting must be at least 14 + 4 x sightings blocks from the player (cap 44).
- **Spawn-area events**: `env_spawn_footprints` (coarse-dirt footprints leading to the spawn, or to the player from stage 4) and `env_spawn_sign` (standing sign whose text follows the phase). Every block is temporary: original state restored after 4.5-6 min / 7.5 min or on server stop, and never over a block the player changed.
- **WORLD-scope events** (Target.GLOBAL, shared cooldown): `world_distant_calls`, `world_static_wave`, `world_hush`, `world_everyone_watched` (reach every living non-spectator player in that dimension; no teleports).
- **Re-modelled originals**: `tools/monsters_orig.py` adds second-pass silhouette parts to all 17 original creatures; geo/animations regenerated with `tools/monsters.py` (safe, does not touch sounds/lang).
- **Keys**: sealed doors in overworld structures no longer need a key nobody can find: they open for a player who has read 3 notes, heard 2 recordings, or has the door/archive hint evidence (keys still work; the dimension maze and House 30 still use real keys found in the world).
- **Removed items**: damaged_recording, cassette_tape, lantern_oil, doorframe_splinter, containment_badge, static_dust, copper_wire_spool, salt_pouch.
## Not done
Everything above is verified by CI only structurally. The in-game feel (looks, sound, distances, balance) and multiplayer death/reconnect behaviour need a real session. tools/preview.py is only a rough silhouette sheet, not a render.
## Tile-rule worlds (all 48 horror dimensions)
`world/gen/Wonder.java` (hand-written; generated ThemeData/ProfileData untouched) turns every horror dimension into a closed, repeating "rooms" world: hallways, houses, libraries, halls, caves, vertical shafts or pillar fields. Dimension names, stories, loot and keys are kept; only the build method changed (cloud realm and volcano are untouched).
Rules per dimension: period (layout repeats every N chunks), lattice (straight corridors every L-th chunk row/column), mono (one material), anomaly (rare unrepeated rooms, never within 2 chunks of the origin), openPlan (pillar fields without walls).
CI checks repetition, door equality between repeats, open corridor rows, and real chunk generation with a bedrock ceiling for every rule. Not verified: how it looks or feels, door/loot balance, whether corridors read as frightening. Existing saves keep their old chunks.
